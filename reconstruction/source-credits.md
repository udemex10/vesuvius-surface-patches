# Source credits and scope of the modifications

PHerc. 125 and PHerc. 268 are held by the Biblioteca Nazionale di Napoli “Vittorio Emanuele III”, Officina dei Papiri Ercolanesi. The official Vesuvius Challenge catalog credits ESRF Grenoble for the scans. PHerc. 125 uses volume 20250821151825 at 9.362 µm and 113 keV. PHerc. 268 uses volume 20251110183117 at 8.640 µm and 116 keV. The organizer’s M7 surface predictions use model 20260413222639, level 0 and threshold 0.2. [PHerc. 125 catalog](https://scrollprize.org/data_browser/PHerc0125) · [PHerc. 268 catalog](https://scrollprize.org/data_browser/PHerc0268)

Credit Giorgio Angelotti and colleagues for *Vesuvius Challenge – CT Scans of Herculaneum Papyri*. The source pages identify CC BY-NC 4.0 for these datasets. The derived coordinates, face masks and geometry visualizations retain the upstream attribution and noncommercial terms. This work traced the supplied surface predictions, tested agreement between routes, discarded unsupported regions, and parameterized the remaining vertices in native CT coordinates. The code can resample original CT at the derived positions. [Dataset citation and authors](https://scrollprize.org/data#how-to-cite) · [Data terms](https://scrollprize.org/data#licenses) · [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

The accompanying methods benchmark uses historical EduceLab-Scrolls CT data from EduceLab and the University of Kentucky. Cite Parsons, Parker, Chapman, Hayashida and Seales (2023) when describing the legacy data. The Vesuvius Challenge’s published harmonized annotations distinguish individual sheets. The notebook-equivalent replay retrieves native source assets from the organizer. The stock GrowPatch reference executable and source are maintained in the ScrollPrize villa repository, and its upstream component license applies separately. [EduceLab-Scrolls publication](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.02084) · [Historical annotation release](https://dl.ash2txt.org/full-scrolls/Scroll1/PHercParis4.volpkg/volumetric-instance-labels/README.txt) · [Volume Cartographer](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/tree/main/volume-cartographer)

The contribution code’s public license is for the contributor to choose. The acquisition-specific upstream data terms and software dependencies retain their own attribution and licenses.

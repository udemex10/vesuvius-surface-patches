# Held-out surface recovery on PHerc. Paris. 4

## Question and public data

Can the new method recover more seed-connected surface at the error rate achieved by stock GrowPatch? The benchmark uses registered CT and the same M7 surface prediction for both arms on the April 2026 PHerc. Paris. 4 scan, in 9.6 µm voxel coordinates. Regional references are manually verified organizer meshes. Interpolation between their original knots limits reference precision. Both methods receive an identical seed and local orientation. Neither method receives the evaluation mesh beyond its small seed neighborhood. The reference might share upstream machine predictions with the evaluated methods. This benchmark measures agreement with vetted surfaces. A discrepancy alone cannot establish that a method jumped to a different winding. [Reference conventions](https://scrollprize.org/data/datasets/spiral-input-PHercParis4-README.md)

## Locked experiment

Select four development, eight test and four confirmation locations from disjoint physical regions before viewing predictions. Hold the final four in reserve while analyzing the first test. Site selection, inputs, exclusions and checksums belong in the dataset manifest. Compare the official GrowPatch binary in this environment against locally implemented reconstruction, with the same physical field of view and inputs. Use development sites to choose between stock presets and to set the acceptance thresholds for the new method. Report both stock configurations and both local variants if available. Do not fit a threshold to a test site's reference. Preserve absent or failed predictions in results.

The primary sample is a disc of radius 96 voxels in the published seed tangent frame, sampled every two voxels. Shoot parallel rays through the structured triangulated surfaces. A site counts only where the vetted reference uniquely defines the sheet in that projection. Exclude reference folds with a normal more than 60 degrees from the seed normal. Weight areas by the physical area of the reference surface. Exclude the shared 12-voxel seed collar from area and error totals. The collar may connect a reconstruction to its seed.

An accepted point is correct when the estimated local perpendicular distance to the reference is at most two voxels (19.2 µm). Treat multiple candidate intersections separated by more than one voxel as erroneous. Report sensitivity at one and four voxels. The primary area is the area of correctly recovered points connected to within four voxels of the seed by a four-neighbor path of correct points. Other correctly predicted islands are reported separately. Missing predictions contribute zero area, with no accepted error rate when an arm emits nothing. Report reference-evaluable area, accepted area, incorrect area, connected correct area, and their site-level differences.

Set the primary risk budget to the winning stock preset's observed development error fraction, weighted by evaluated area. The preset with the largest total seed-connected correct area is the stock comparator, with lower error breaking a tie. For the local methods use common confidence thresholds from 0.00 through 1.00 in steps of 0.05. Select the candidate with the largest connected correct area satisfying the stock error budget on development. Also test a stock-first extension which appends seed-connected candidate surface only where stock has no output and the two surfaces agree along the seam; the extension must use only the public seed and predictions. If no candidate has usable coverage within the budget, record a failed development gate and explicitly label subsequent results exploratory.

On the eight untouched sites, a positive point estimate requires both greater total connected correct area and an observed error fraction no greater than stock. Report the number of sites improved, unchanged, and worse. Use a paired region bootstrap to show uncertainty, keeping all pixels of each sampled region together. With few sites on one scroll, these intervals describe local variation only. A top-prize claim would need independent replication on other material. If the initial result warrants confirmation, freeze the method and evaluate the reserved four without altering it. Any revised method evaluated on the original eight uses them as development evidence.

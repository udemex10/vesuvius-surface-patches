# Local refinement after the first two comparisons

The first development, test and confirmation results are known for the original 16 Paris. 4 patches and 16 historical labeled cubes. These sites may inform new hypotheses. Only the separately locked pressure-test patches and additional historical instance cubes can validate this follow-up. The first comparisons and their unfavorable results stay in the record.

The proposed refinement keeps the stock chart wherever it cannot confidently place the sheet more precisely. Test three low-cost corrections: a subvoxel shift toward the midpoint of the nearest bounded input-prediction band along the shared seed normal; a shift toward a nearby independent graph reconstruction with a maximum allowed discrepancy; and a half-strength seed correction that decays with distance. The intensity-band test uses the upstream foreground only and never reads instance labels. Reject open, wide, or near-tied neighboring bands. Limit corrections to a few voxels. These methods refine coordinates within a local mesh; detecting an offset does not prove a switch to a different papyrus sheet.

Choose among fixed rules on prior development data only and keep alternatives visible. On the pressure cohort compare to previously selected stock with the oriented eight-voxel initialization. On historical cubes, compare to the official stock configuration with the best development connected correct area among configurations whose development unsafe fraction does not exceed the original stock's and whose known wrong-sheet fraction also does not exceed it. Include a locked threshold-128 stock comparison and a threshold-192 sensitivity if available. If a historical comparator has zero known wrong-sheet errors, an increase fails that safety criterion. Summarize both region-level and pooled changes; the additional historical cubes share a scroll and annotation campaign with the earlier cohort.

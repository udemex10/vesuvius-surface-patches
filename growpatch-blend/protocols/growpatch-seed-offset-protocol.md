# Seed offset and connectivity sensitivity

This follow-up was specified after seeing the four development cases and before inspecting the eight test or four confirmation scores. In one development case the default evaluation assigned stock zero seed-connected area even though much of its surface away from the seed agreed with the vetted reference. A few voxels of offset at the shared seed could cause this result. The original frozen experiment remains the primary comparison.

Check two alternatives. The recovery program can use the difference between the shared seed and the local center of the M7 surface band to preserve the seed's position within that band as the sheet grows. Stock receives a separate post-processing control using its own emitted mesh. In the shared seed tangent frame, subtract the median signed stock height within six voxels of the seed, capped at three voxels in either direction. A correction needs five unique rays with one well-defined crossing. Evaluation still excludes the seed collar. Report original and adjusted stock separately. This also tests whether a small change to the established tool accounts for an apparent advantage.

Select the offset-preserving recovery configuration using the four development sites, then test that configuration on all untouched sites without per-site adjustment using references. Alongside the original seed-connected metric, report the surface area of the largest correct component anywhere, the area connected to the 12-voxel seed collar, total correct area, and estimated errors on the shared footprint. Publish one- and four-voxel sensitivity alongside the two-voxel result. A gain that depends on a single seed or tolerance is weaker evidence than consistent changes in physical sheet identity.

# Screening stock's output at fixed risk budgets

This secondary experiment was specified before opening the Paris. 4 test scores. Compare the stock internal surface-snap loss with a check based on whether a separate reconstruction agrees with stock. Where the separate method emits a surface, measure the absolute difference of the two predicted heights along the shared seed normal. A large difference indicates less confidence. Where it emits nothing the disagreement check abstains. Use the original frozen two-route variant. Evaluate unchanged stock, the stock-only loss score, and disagreement-gated stock. A matching ray is still judged against the held-out reference. Both estimates can miss the same physical sheet.

Use 1%, 2%, 5%, 10% and the development stock error as risk budgets. Within each arm take a grid of score cutoffs at development percentiles from 0 to 100 in steps of five percent, plus unrestricted acceptance. Select by maximum total seed-connected correct area on development under each ceiling. Require positive accepted area and record an unavailable operating point if no threshold satisfies the budget. Freeze the cutoffs before scoring the test and confirmation regions. Compare arms using their actual risk and area on each split. The 5% budget is the primary sensitivity operating point. Changing a safety threshold is useful only when it preserves additional correct, connected geometry at comparable observed error.
